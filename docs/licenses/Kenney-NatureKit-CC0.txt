Pack contents (330 files): low-poly trees, palms, rocks, stones, bushes, flowers, grass,
mushroom and plant models, in GLTF / FBX / OBJ. The OBJ models are the ones the game loads.

The Nature Kit models carry no texture: each one is a handful of flat material colours
(`Kd`) in its MTL, so they are drawn with the shared white map tinted by each group's own
colour.

Shipped as: the models actually placed are copied into
app/src/main/assets/models/nature/ (cactus_short, cactus_tall, plant_bush, plant_bushLarge,
plant_bushSmall, grass, grass_large, rock_largeA, tree_palm, with their .mtl files). The
12 MB source archive is kept out of the APK; the open-source kit stays in assets-source/.
