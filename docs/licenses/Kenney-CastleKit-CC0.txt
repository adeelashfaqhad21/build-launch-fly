Pack contents (75 files, version 2.0): a modular low-poly medieval set — wall sections,
towers, gates, stairs, roofs, doors, windows, banners and flags — in GLTF / FBX / OBJ with a
single shared palette texture. The OBJ models are the ones the game loads.

Intended use in this project: the timber/stone hangar shell, its open front wall and roof,
plus the ruins and stone-arch obstacles along the flight course.

Shipped as: app/src/main/assets/kits/kenney_castle.zip
Read in memory at load time (gfx/ZipKit); no build-time unpack step.
