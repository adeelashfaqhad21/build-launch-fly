Downloads supplied in assets-source/Planes /:

1. "samolet" by vrlab development
   https://poly.pizza/m/6OgsLgd7vKM
   Tagged #Biplane. **This is the one shipped in the game**, as
   app/src/main/assets/aircraft/biplane.zip.

2. "Airplane" by Poly by Google
   https://poly.pizza/m/8VysVKMXN2J
   Not used.

3. "very cute airplane" by Akash Rudra
   https://poly.pizza/m/3UtIosDm9u-
   Not used.

Format: OBJ / GLTF. The engine reads the OBJ and PNG entries straight out of the zip at
runtime (gfx/ZipKit + gfx/PropLoader), so no build-time unpack step is needed.
