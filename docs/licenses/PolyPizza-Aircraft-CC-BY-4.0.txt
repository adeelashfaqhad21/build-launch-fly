Downloads supplied in assets-source/Planes /:

1. "samolet" by vrlab development
   https://poly.pizza/m/6OgsLgd7vKM
   Tagged #Biplane. **This is the aircraft the game flies**, shipped as
   app/src/main/assets/aircraft/samolet/model.obj + materials.mtl.

2. "Airplane" by Poly by Google
   https://poly.pizza/m/8VysVKMXN2J
   Unzipped in assets-source; kept as a candidate second airframe.

3. "very cute airplane" by Akash Rudra
   https://poly.pizza/m/3UtIosDm9u-
   Not used.

Format: OBJ. "samolet" has no texture and no UVs — its seven colours are flat `Kd` values in
materials.mtl — so the engine splits it by material (aircraft/AircraftModelLoader) and draws
each part with the shared white map tinted by that colour. One welded mesh: there are no
separate wing, tail or propeller objects.
