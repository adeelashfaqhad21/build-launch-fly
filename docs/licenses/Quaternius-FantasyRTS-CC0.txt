Quaternius — Ultimate Fantasy RTS Pack
======================================

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatefantasyrts.html
Download: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1h7sztlZyavWla-JDk3jp6KiWDdMh08yd
Author: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)

The folder is the Ultimate Fantasy RTS pack: buildings, walls, farms, towers,
market, temple, mine, port, mountains, crates and barrels. Each model is an
OBJ with a matching PNG texture.

Downloaded (OBJ + PNG) and shipped in app/src/main/assets/buildings:
  Houses_FirstAge_1_Level2, Windmill_FirstAge, WatchTower_FirstAge_Level2,
  Dock_FirstAge, Mine, TowerHouse_FirstAge, TownCenter_FirstAge_Level1,
  Mountain_Single, MountainLarge_Single, Crate, Barrel

They are loaded by gfx/PropLoader (one merged mesh per model, the PNG as
albedo, normalised to a target height with its base on the ground) and placed
as static scenery per realm. The remaining RTS models stay in the Drive
folder; this is a curated subset.

CC0 places the work in the public domain, so no attribution is required in
the app. This record is kept for provenance.
