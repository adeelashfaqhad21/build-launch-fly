Files shipped in app/src/main/assets/quaternius/ (OBJ + leaf/rock PNG maps):
  BirchTree_1.obj, BirchTree_Leaves.png
  MapleTree_1.obj, MapleTree_Leaves.png
  PineTree_1.obj,  PineTree_Leaves.png
  PalmTree_1.obj,  PalmTree_Leaves.png

  Flower_1_Clump.obj, Flowers.png
  Grass_Large.obj, Grass.png
  Rock_1.obj,      Rocks.png

Modifications: the models are loaded at runtime by gfx/ObjLoader and normalised
(1 unit tall, base at y = 0, centred in XZ); trunk/bark geometry is drawn with
the game's procedurally generated bark texture instead of the pack's 20 MB
4K bark map, which is not shipped. No other edits were made to the assets.

CC0 places the work in the public domain, so no attribution is required in the
app. This record is kept for provenance.
