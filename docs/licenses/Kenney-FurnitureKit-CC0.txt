Pack contents (140 files): low-poly interior furniture — benches, chairs, stools, tables,
desks, sofas, beds, shelving, boxes, lamps, plants, rugs and a few room modules — in GLTF /
FBX / OBJ / DAE / STL. The OBJ models are the ones the game loads.

The models carry no texture: each one is a handful of flat material colours (`Kd`) in its
MTL, so they are split by material at load time and drawn with the shared white map tinted by
each group's own colour.

Shipped as: the props actually placed are copied into app/src/main/assets/models/furniture/
(table, bench, stoolBar, cardboardBoxClosed, trashcan, with their .mtl files) and stood on
the hangar floor by world/HangarMesh. The source pack stays out of the APK and out of the
repository (assets-source is ignored).
