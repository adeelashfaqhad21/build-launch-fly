Quaternius — Ultimate Animated Animals Pack
==========================================

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html
Download: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uJ3N5HfB7jKTseJUNQr3N4YaN0UuEtHk
Author: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The pack holds 12 rigged animals (Alpaca, Bull, Cow, Deer, Donkey, Fox,
Horse, Horse_White, Husky, ShibaInu, Stag, Wolf), each with 12+ skeletal
animations. All 12 OBJ/MTL pairs and the licence/preview are downloaded in
assets-source/animals/.

Shipped in app/src/main/assets/animals/ (static OBJ + flat-colour MTL):
  Deer, Donkey, Husky, Cow, Stag

The OBJ models are used in a static pose: the animations are skeletal and
this renderer has no skinning, so clips cannot play. Colours come from the
MTL `Kd` values; the pack carries no texture maps for these meshes.

CC0 places the work in the public domain, so no attribution is required in
the app. This record is kept for provenance.
