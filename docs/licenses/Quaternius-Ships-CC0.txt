Quaternius — Ships Pack
======================

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ships.html
Download: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Qf31QTnGfxRzYxx8dHmlVGDT4KmIa0Vy
Author: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Six models: Boat, BoatWSail, CruiseShip, Lifeboat, Sail ship, Viking boat.
All are downloaded in assets-source/ships (OBJ/MTL, plus licence and
preview). The five used in the ocean realm are shipped in
app/src/main/assets/ships with space-free names:

  BoatWSail.obj/.mtl, CruiseShip.obj/.mtl, Lifeboat.obj/.mtl,
  SailShip.obj/.mtl, VikingBoat.obj/.mtl

The ships are static meshes with flat MTL colours (no texture maps). They are
normalised to a target height and anchored offshore at the water line, with a
slow bob applied by WorldScene.

CC0 places the work in the public domain, so no attribution is required in
the app. This record is kept for provenance.
